FAQ

What services do you offer?
I offer Design & Discovery, Design & Build (code handoff or ongoing maintenance), and Performance & Accessibility audits.
How long does it take?
Most projects run 2–4 weeks depending on scope and content readiness. Audits typically take 1–2 weeks.
Do I need to know code?
Nope—share your goals and content and I’ll handle the build. I include a short handoff so you’re comfortable updating.
Can you work with my existing site?
Yes. I can improve performance, accessibility, and UX, or add new components while keeping your current stack.
What do you need to get started?
A quick brief (goals, audience, examples), brand assets (logo, colors, fonts), and access to hosting/repos if we’re iterating on an existing site.
Do you provide hosting/CMS recommendations?
Absolutely. I frequently deploy to Vercel/Netlify and can set up a simple CMS flow, or keep it code-only—whatever fits your team.
